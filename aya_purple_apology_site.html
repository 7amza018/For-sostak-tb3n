Aya, I want to sincerely apologize for my actions and for the improper things I said. I know that words and actions can hurt, especially when someone matters so much. I'm sorry for the moments when I said something bad, didn't give you enough attention, or made you feel like I wasn't really there for you.

And honestly, whenever you're around, I somehow end up laughing with you. I love that your personality can be so angry and so cute at the same time. 💜

I also remember that talking about the things you love can make you happy. I want to listen more, pay more attention, and understand you better.
